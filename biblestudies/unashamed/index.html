Verbum

Unashamed: Standing Strong in Your Faith

14-18

Intro: Base scripture

For I am not ashamed of the Good News of Christ, because it is the power of God for salvation for everyone who believes, for the Jew first, and also for the Greek.
Romans 1:16 WEB

Paul wrote this while living in a culture that often mocked or persecuted Christians. Yet he boldly declared he wasn’t ashamed. Why? Because the gospel isn’t weak — it’s powerful.

1: You will feel pressure - but God gives us courage

For God didn’t give us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and self-control.
2 Timothy 1:7 WEB

High school can feel intimidating. You may feel pressure to:
-stay silent about your beleifs.
-Compromise to fit in
-Laugh at things you know aren’t right

But fear does not come from God. Power, love, and self-control do.



Haven’t I commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Don’t be afraid. Don’t be dismayed, for Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go.
Joshua 1:9 WEB

God didn’t promise comfort — He promised His presence.

2: Your Life Speaks Louder Than Your Words

You are the light of the world... let your light shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father who is in heaven.
Matthew 5:14-16 WEB

Being unashamed doesn’t mean being loud or argumentative. It means living differently.

-When others gossip, you choose kindness.

-When others cheat, you choose integrity.

-When others tear down, you build up.



Let no man despise your youth; but be an example to those who believe, in word, in your way of life, in love, in spirit, in faith, and in purity.
1 Timothy 4:12

Your age does not limit your influence.

3: Your Identity Is Secure

Sometimes we’re ashamed because we’re unsure of who we are.

But Romans 8:38–39 says nothing can separate us from the love of God.



Therefore if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old things have passed away. Behold, all things have become new.
2 Corinthians 5:17 WEB

You are not defined by:

-Your mistakes

-Your friend group

-Your followers

-Your popularity

You are defined by Christ.



When you know who you belong to, you don’t need approval from everyone else.

Discussion/reflection

Read Romans 1:16 again. What do you think Paul risked by saying this publicly?

In 2 Timothy 1:7, which word stands out most to you — power, love, or self-discipline? Why?

How can you be “light” according to Matthew 5:16 at school this week?

What does 1 Timothy 4:12 say about your age and influence?

Which verse in this study challenges you the most?

Weekly challenge

Memorize Romans 1:16

Pray through Joshua 1:9 each morning before school

Choose one action this week that reflects Matthew 5:16

Closing prayer (optional)

“God, Your Word says You have not given me a spirit of fear. Help me live unashamed of the gospel. Make me a light at my school. Give me courage, because You promise to be with me wherever I go. Amen.”
